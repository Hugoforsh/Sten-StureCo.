About

I care about
your song. Genuinely.

My name is Sten-Sture. I've been making music for nine years — producing, mixing, mastering — and the thing that's never changed is how much I care about getting it right. Not just technically right. Emotionally right. The mix that makes you feel the song the way you felt it when you first wrote it.

I'm based in Södermalm, Stockholm, and I love working with artists face to face. If you're in the city, come into the studio. Sit with the speakers, hear it in the room, tell me what you're feeling. Some of my favourite mixes have come from those conversations — the ones you can't have over email.

For artists further away, we work remotely — but I always make time for a proper call before we begin. I want to understand the song, not just the stems.

The process

It starts with a conversation.

01
We talk about the song
Before anything else, I want to understand what you've made and where you want to take it. A short call, a voice note, a few emails — whatever feels natural. This is where I start listening, before the stems even arrive.
02
In the studio — together or apart
If you're in Stockholm, come in. Sit with the monitors, drink coffee, and hear your music in the room. If you're further away, send your stems and I'll mix with the same care — just without the coffee on your end.
03
You hear it — and we talk again
I'll send a private link to stream the first mix. Then I want to hear what you think — not just "can you turn up the snare" but how it feels. Two revision rounds are included. I've never once minded a third if something isn't right.
04
Your music, ready to go
When you're happy — genuinely happy — I deliver the final files. WAV and MP3, clearly named, ready for mastering or straight to release. That's it. No invoice surprises, no chasing.
